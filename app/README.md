# Advocate — an agent for a river with rights

**Idea card.** Some bodies of water are legal persons: the Whanganui River (NZ, 2017), the Atrato (Colombia, 2016), the Mar Menor
lagoon (Spain, Ley 19/2022). They have guardians but no analyst. Advocate is an agent whose only client is such a body of water:
it reads every open dataset about it, finds pending decisions and worsening trends, tests competing causes with numbers, and
drafts the objection or notice its human guardians may file. It never files anything itself.

**Why now.** Rights-of-nature statutes are spreading faster than the capacity to use them; the data to defend a lagoon already
exists in five agencies' portals and nobody joins it.

**Question.** *Which pending decisions and trends threaten the Mar Menor, why, and what objection should its guardians file?*

## What the agent does
| step | what | how |
|---|---|---|
| Scout | open decisions ≤ 15 km with a deadline inside 90 days; parameters at the client's own stations breaching or trending (±15 % in 12 months) | SQL over `decisions`, `measurements`, `thresholds` |
| Investigator | trend → agricultural runoff · urban discharge · groundwater abstraction · natural variation; decision → increases nutrient load · increases abstraction · no material effect | fixed competing sets |
| Skeptic | inflow-leads-lagoon cross-correlation (lag 1–3 months) + permitted fertiliser tonnage within 10 km; discharge permits + chlorophyll response; groundwater trend + abstraction volume; 12-month slope vs 2× the 2019–21 seasonal amplitude; keyword + distance rules for decisions | numpy over SQL results, every verdict carries its SQL |
| Planner | draft objection / notice (addressee, legal basis, facts with numbers, request), monitoring request, watch | `action.props["objection"]`, status *draft* until a guardian approves |
| Explainer | plain-language summary for the guardians | Nemotron via Meterless (offline templater without a key) |

## Data
Seed CSVs under `app/data/seed/` are **illustrative** (generated by `make_seed_illustrative.py`; real places and bodies, invented
numbers). Real sources: Ley 19/2022 and Ley 3/2020 (legal basis), CHS open data (permits, gauges), EEA Waterbase/WISE (water
quality; `fetch_eea_waterbase.py`), BORM/municipal notices (decisions; `fetch_chs_opendata.py` documents the columns).
`app/schema.sql` creates the same tables in Postgres for registration in Zetaris.

## Demo script (90 s)
1. Open the UI: client Mar Menor, three signals on the map — a fertiliser-plan renewal 3 km from the shore, a nitrate rise at the
   lagoon centre above the legal limit, a residential plan 12 km out.
2. Run agent. The nitrate rise: inflow leads the lagoon by 2 months (correlation shown), 1,200 t/yr nitrogen permitted nearby →
   agricultural runoff **supported**; seasonality, discharge and groundwater **rejected**, each with its number. Hover a verdict: the SQL.
3. The fertiliser renewal: *increases nutrient load* supported → **Draft objection** with addressee, legal basis, facts, request.
   The residential plan: *no material effect* → "No objection". The agent says no when the data says no.
4. Press Approve on the objection: status flips, the ledger logs the human. Re-run: memory skips 7 rejected hypotheses (warm run).

## Sponsor mapping
Zetaris federates water-quality series, permits and planning notices from different agencies in one SQL door; Meterless routes
every model call and keeps run memory; Nemotron writes the guardians' explanation; built in Cursor.

## Limits and framing
Drafts for human guardians, never autonomous filing. Causal tests are screening rules, not proof; the objection text asks for an
impact assessment, it does not assert liability. Seed numbers are invented; swap in the fetched series before any public demo.
